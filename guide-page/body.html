      About 15 minutes
      
Set it up

    

    

      

        
Get your copy

        
Everything is on GitHub: github.com/BuildingBrian/mba-internship-radar.

        
    
          
  1. Daily on GitHub (recommended): click Use this template, make it private, and add your my-radar.json. It scans every morning by itself, with nothing to install.
    2. 
          
  2. Claude Code: download the folder, unzip it, open it in Claude Code on your computer, say "Set up my internship radar".
    3. 
          
  3. Claude app: download internship-radar-skill.zip. Turn on Settings → Capabilities → Code execution and file creation, then go to Customize → Skills → + → Create skill → Upload a skill. It builds your profile; the scan itself runs on GitHub or in Claude Code.
    4. 
        

      

      

        
Paste your profile

        
On GitHub, paste the "my-radar.json" tab as a new file named my-radar.json, then open Actions → Internship Radar → Run workflow. Your first list arrives in about a minute.

        
In Claude Code, say "Set up my internship radar" and paste the "For Claude" text above. Claude builds your company list and runs your first scan in about 30 seconds. The Claude app can build your profile the same way, but its sandbox can't reach job boards to scan.

      

      

        
Turn on alerts

        
Pick a channel below. Passwords go into GitHub Secrets or a radar.env file on your Mac, never into a chat. Then run test-alert to see one arrive. The repo's ALERTS.md and SOURCES.md walk through each one.

      Know the limits
      
What it covers

    

    

      
Job boards
166 company boards checked daily: tech, AI, robotics, defense, aerospace, fintech, climate and media, plus the big healthcare and consumer companies on Workday or Greenhouse. Add any company in one line.

      
LinkedIn, Handshake, CMG
Through the alert emails they already send you, the cmg tool's copy of CMG, or a paste into Claude. Nothing signs in to them, which keeps your accounts safe.

      
Check by hand
77 more companies. Most banks, McKinsey, BCG and Bain, and Google, Microsoft, Apple and Meta post on their own sites. The radar lists them every Monday. CMG stays your main source for on-campus recruiting.
